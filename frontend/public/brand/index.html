01 · Give the capybara room

Use the original transparent artwork. Keep clear space of at least one ear-height on every side. Do not stretch, rotate, or add accessories.

02 · Keep the name simple

Lowercase capybara in the wordmark. Capybara in sentences. usecapybara.com for the domain. The dot belongs to the visual wordmark.

03 · Warmth with purpose

Oat for breathing room, cocoa for text, pond for actions. Use sage behind the animal. Pair financial status colors with explicit labels and figures.
